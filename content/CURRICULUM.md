# Dualinguage curriculum outline

One outline serves every course. Each unit is written once in `content/src/<level>/uNN.json` with all languages side by side, so
English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Amazigh and German stay in step. Amazigh and German stop after B3.

Each level has 8 units. A unit is 4 learn lessons, 1 conversation and 1 unit review. Levels end with a checkpoint (planned for a later engine step).

| Level | Stage | Rough CEFR | Can do at the end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | Beginner | Pre-A1 | greet, introduce yourself, count to 10, name food, colours, days, animals, places |
| B2 | Beginner | A1 | talk about yourself, numbers to 100, prices, order food, tell time, weather, clothes, hobbies |
| B3 | Beginner | A2 | describe a day, ask the way, use past and future, say what hurts, shop and make requests |
| M1 | Middle | A2+ to B1 | travel, book a room, tell a story in the past, make plans, describe people and places |
| M2 | Middle | B1 | talk about work, see a doctor, give opinions, compare, technology, study, money |
| M3 | Middle | B1+ to B2 | follow the news, tell longer stories, relationships, environment, hypotheticals, discussions |
| A1 | Advanced | B2 | debate, culture and arts, media, society, history, science, sport, formal and informal register |
| A2 | Advanced | B2+ to C1 | meetings, negotiation, research, law, economy, formal writing, academia, innovation |
| A3 | Advanced | C1 | idioms, nuance, literature, politics, ideas, long reading, humour and irony, capstone |

## Notes and limits

- **Script units are not built yet.** Arabic and Amazigh B1 should open with an alphabet and sounds unit. That needs a letter-recognition exercise type, which the app does not have yet, so it is planned with the engine work, not as plain lessons.
- **Amazigh** follows Standard Moroccan Amazigh in Tifinagh with Latin transliteration. A unit appears in the Amazigh course only when it has at least 6 confident words, so some units are shorter or missing until a native speaker fills the gaps.
- **Arabic** is Modern Standard Arabic with transliteration under the text. No vowel marks yet.
- **German** and **English** are written in the same pass as the others. Native checks are still advised.
- **Word counts**: B1 to B3 aim for about 100 words per unit later on; the pilot units are 12 words to prove the pipeline.
